Iteration-level scheduling, chunked prefill, hash-based prefix caching, and a paged KV cache keep inference moving. CUDA graph capture and replay accelerate decoding.

I reimplemented the model in PyTorch, added a meta-device Safetensors loader with fused QKV and SwiGLU weights, and integrated FlashAttention-2. In project benchmarks, throughput was within 10% of vLLM.
